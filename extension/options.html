PhishGuard AI settings

Protection

Screen watch (page analysis)

Looks at the structure of each page - login forms that send data to another site, misleading links, hidden frames - to catch phishing that a URL alone cannot reveal. It never reads what you type, cookies, page text or screenshots. Needs permission to read pages, which Chrome will ask you to confirm.
